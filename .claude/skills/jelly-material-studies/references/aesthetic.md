# Aesthetic Language — Material Studies Series

All scenes share a quiet, editorial, tactile "material study" aesthetic. Not a game. Think studio specimen or diorama on paper.

## Page

- Background: warm paper `#ece9e3`
- Ink: `#241f1d`
- Rules / subtle: `#d5d0c8`
- Accent: typically turquoise `#12a2a6` or `#17a39b` (varies by scene)
- Typography:
  - Display title: large italic serif (Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Georgia, or system serif)
  - Eyebrow: small uppercase sans, tracking wide — "MATERIAL STUDIES"
  - UI: clean sans
  - Numbers: mono

## Layout

- Top-left masthead:
  - Eyebrow "MATERIAL STUDIES"
  - Huge italic H1 "Subject / Jelly." (line break intentional)
  - Short rule
  - One-line poetic caption
- Top-right: status pill with green/red dot + "WEBGPU · LIVE" (or Paused / Unavailable)
- Right side: frosted glass control panel
- Bottom-left: "HOW TO PLAY" in italic + one grey line of gesture hints
- Center: the diorama or specimen
- Mobile: control panel becomes a bottom sheet, touch orbit/pinch, no page scroll

## The Block (Dioramas)

- Square cut-out block (typically 5.2 × 5.2 units)
- Camera at ~3/4 orbit from the front corner
- Cut faces show candy-coloured soil strata (sand, shell band, caramel, chocolate bedrock)
- Sea/jelly surface and walls are translucent with refraction, absorption, caustics, light shafts, foam lace, and a bright meniscus on cut edges

## Soft-Body Specimens

- Studio cyclorama or simple plate
- Soft PCSS shadows tinted by the jelly
- Contact AO
- Subsurface, Fresnel, sugar-crystal glints

## Tone

Quiet. Editorial. Tactile. The jelly should feel like something you could almost pick up and squish.
